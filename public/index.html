How the RentAPog Affiliate System Works

At RentAPog, we believe in rewarding our community. Our affiliate program is designed to maximize your earnings and help you build a sustainable, growing income stream. Here’s how it works:

  • 100% Commissions: You keep every dollar from your referrals—except for your second sale at each package tier.
  • Pass-Up System: Your second sale at each tier is automatically passed up to the admin. This keeps the platform sustainable and ensures everyone benefits.

  • Multi-Tiered: Each package tier ($29, $49, $99, etc.) tracks sales independently. You can earn at every level you own.

  • Sub-Admin Option: Become a sub-admin and keep your second sales, just like a franchise owner.
